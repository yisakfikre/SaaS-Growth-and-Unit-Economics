Checkpoint 6 — run all three sub-tests, not just a glance at the totals
_MRR Share of Total's ALLSELECTED() logic has only ever been validated against Page 2's Plan/Segment parameter. This page introduces two contexts it has never been tested under — a three-option Group By parameter, and arrival via drillthrough — either of which could change what ALLSELECTED() actually resolves to. Do not accept "the numbers looked roughly right" as a pass; add up the percentages by hand for at least one case in each row below.
  1. Baseline, no selection. Shares sum to exactly 100% across all five plans, company-wide.
  2. Via Group By selection (no drillthrough). On Page 5 itself, with Group By = Industry, click the Software bubble in V1. Note V2's five share values, add them by hand. They must sum to 100% within Software — not company-wide, and not frozen at the pre-click baseline. Repeat once with Group By = Country to confirm the same holds for a different underlying field.
  3. Via drillthrough arrival. From Page 2, drill through on one segment. On arrival, before clicking anything else on Page 5, check V2 immediately. Its shares must already sum to 100% within the drilled segment — confirming the drillthrough filter reaches _MRR Share of Total's denominator logic the same way a normal in-page click does, not just the numerator.
If any of the three sums to something other than 100%, the measure is resolving its denominator against the wrong filter scope for that context — stop and report which of the three failed before proceeding to Section 7.

Checkpoint 8 — confirm the filter itself, not just its visible effect
Seeing one bubble and one row on arrival is consistent with the drill-through working correctly — but it's also consistent with a broken link that happens to show a plausible-looking page. Confirm the actual filter, not just the downstream appearance.
  1. On Page 2, set the Plan/Segment parameter to Segment, right-click a segment row (e.g. Enterprise) → "Drillthrough → Segment Analysis" appears. Set the parameter to Plan, right-click a plan row → no Page 5 option appears (expected — DimPlan carries no segment value in that context). On Page 1's MRR-by-Plan bar, right-click any bar → no Page 5 option appears (expected, same reason).
  2. Drill through on Enterprise. On arrival, open the Filters pane and expand "Filters on this page." Confirm it shows DimCustomer[CustomerSegment] is Enterprise explicitly — not a blank filter card, not a different field, not "(All)". This is the direct evidence the link fired correctly, independent of what the visuals happen to display.
  3. With that filter card open, confirm V1 shows exactly one bubble and V3 exactly one row, and that both are labeled Enterprise — the visual outcome, now cross-checked against the confirmed filter rather than assumed from it.
  4. Switch Group By to Industry without touching the Filters pane. Confirm the CustomerSegment is Enterprise filter card is still present and unchanged, and that V1/V3 now show multiple industries — all of them plausibly Enterprise-segment industries, not the full company list.
  5. Click Back. Confirm Page 2 returns with its own parameter selection and any prior state intact, and that Page 5's filter card is gone on the next fresh arrival (i.e. it isn't sticking across visits).
If step 2's filter card shows anything other than the exact expected field and value, stop — the drill-through field well configuration in Section 8 needs to be rechecked before trusting anything else on this page.
